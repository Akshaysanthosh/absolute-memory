Absolute Memory Chrome extension

1. Sign in at your Absolute Memory instance.
2. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
3. Click Load unpacked and select the folder containing manifest.json.
4. Open the extension options, enter your instance address, and approve access to that origin.
5. Match the pairing code, then click Connect Chrome in the app.
6. Pin the extension, open an article, and click Save. You can assign Alt+Shift+S in Chrome.

The default address is http://localhost:3000. Revoke connections from Capture channels in the app.
